  • Explainability Engine: TreeSHAP (SHAP values & feature importance)
  • Visualization: Client-side Plotly.js charts & Matplotlib plots

Decision Threshold Strategy

The production threshold is pinned to 0.28 under the Balanced Strategy (Precision ≥ 50%). This guarantees at least 1 in 2 flagged alerts represents actionable, confirmed fraud while capturing 89.1% of all fraudulent provider activity in the population.
